Engineering approach

From concept to communication.

My work emphasizes the full design process: understanding the problem, developing a solution, communicating it clearly, and building toward a reliable result. I am especially interested in manufacturing, engineering systems, and the components that make products work.

CAD + DrawingPart modeling, assemblies, dimensioned drawings, and clear technical communication.
Hands-on DesignPhysical prototyping, fabrication, iteration, and learning from the finished result.
Systems ThinkingConnecting mechanisms, electronics, software, and user needs into one working system.
Professional GrowthBuilding the practical judgment, precision, and collaboration expected of a mechanical engineer.
