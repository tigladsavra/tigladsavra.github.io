Personal statement

About Me

Hello, my name is Tiglad Savra, and I am a mechanical engineering student at San José State University. I am interested in the relationship between design, manufacturing, and the mechanical systems that allow products to function reliably.

Through my academic and hands-on engineering projects, I have developed experience with technical drawings, CAD-based design, fabrication, machining, prototyping, and assembly. I enjoy taking an initial concept and developing it into a physical result that can be tested, evaluated, and improved.

My interests include manufacturing, engineering systems, mechanical components, and design development. I am especially motivated by the process of understanding how individual parts work together within a larger system. Each project has strengthened my attention to detail, problem-solving ability, technical communication, and confidence working through unfamiliar challenges.

As I continue developing as an engineer, I am building both my technical knowledge and professional experience. This portfolio documents my progress, creativity, and approach to engineering: combining careful design with hands-on work to turn ideas into functional solutions.
